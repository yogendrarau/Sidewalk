# SIDEWALK — 80-Second Judge Demo

Target: **79 seconds including screen transitions** at a calm demo pace. Only the quoted text is spoken; bracketed directions are operator cues.

## Before presenting

- Open the role-selection screen and keep a second browser or phone ready for the buyer view.
- Use the seeded fictional Rosa session and the bundled summons and menu-photo fixtures.
- Use the explicitly labeled sample-store path unless the prepared Shopify test store has passed preflight.
- Keep every screen loaded before speaking; do not wait on venue Wi-Fi during the narration.
